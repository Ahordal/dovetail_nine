DOVETAIL-9 · MAIN HULL · SIDE VIEW · DRAFT

Hull section, decks 01 to 12

Looking at the side of the hull where the docking bay opens. Drawn to scale: 400 m at its longest, 12 decks of about 8 m each. The profile is stepped like the exterior image: tiers on top, full length through the middle decks where the array attaches, modules hanging below.

SETTLED

  • Hull about 400 m long, 90 to 100 m tall.
  • 12 decks, about 5 m floor to ceiling, service spaces between.
  • Decks are shared. The bay and the tram take a section of their decks; the rest holds other rooms and corridors.
  • Tram runs the full length on Deck 06 only.
  • Lifts between decks, access shafts vertical and horizontal, corridors on every deck.

PROPOSED

  • Decks numbered from the top. Deck 01 under the antennas.
  • Hull about 100 m wide, roughly square in cross-section. The bay reaches ~70 m in, nose first.
  • Core Chamber at the middle of the hull, Decks 05 to 07, the black hole at the middle of Deck 06.
  • Deck 06 is the spine: keel, conduits out to both arrays, and the tram passing beside the chamber.
  • Engineering around the chamber, 137 to 263 m along on Decks 05 to 07, meeting both lift shafts, with two vertical access shafts through it, one either side of the chamber.
  • Two lift cores at about 135 m and 265 m, the only positions that reach all 12 decks.
  • SIGMA's core: a 5 m column on the chamber's axis, from Deck 04 up into command on Deck 01, operations room at its base.
  • Experiment control on Deck 05, a shared Science and Engineering room just inside engineering, with R&D next door and a door from each.
  • Shielding plant on Deck 07 in engineering: capacitor Banks A and B either side of the chamber, Valdez's own section. Emitters spread along the conduits and around the crewed sections; the bay sits outside the shield.
  • Stepped profile. Decks 05 to 08 run the full 400 m; decks above and below get shorter. Exact lengths are a first guess.
  • Docking bay across Decks 08 to 11, centred along the hull under the superstructure, unpressurized, open to space, about 98 m long.
  • Docking ring: the clamps inside the bay that hold the ship.
  • Airlock B on Deck 09 in the bay wall, joined to the ship by a boarding umbilical. Airlock A faces it across the bay, its own umbilical pulled back.

  • Access shaft routes.
  • Which end is forward, where the array focuses its node.
  • Needs a dedicated place: array access at both ends, command and comms, life support, hydroponics.
  • Everything else: R&D, quarters, galley, medical, hydroponics, administration.
