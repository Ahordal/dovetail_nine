# Dovetail-9 Canon

The settled facts of the setting. When a lore file and this document disagree, this document wins and the lore file gets fixed.

Status markers: **[settled]** = decided, **[proposed]** = suggestion awaiting approval, **[open]** = undecided.

---

## Tone & influences

- **Alien: Isolation** shapes the station's lore and environment: retro-futurist, analog-heavy corporate tech, a decaying station, and a story told through terminals and crew emails.
- **Alien: Romulus** is also a reference.
- **Pseudo-science, not magic.** FTL, corridors, and the anomalies are explained in plausible technical language with consistent rules. Nothing supernatural, even when it feels like a ghost story.

- **2477**, ten years after Dovetail-9 vanished in 2467.

## Tobias Quell

- **Name:** Tobias Quell. Never "Quill". **[settled]**
- **Age:** 42 at the start of the story, so born **2435**. **[settled]**
- **Title:** No doctorate. Not "Dr." anywhere in copy or files. **[settled]**
- **Background:** Born on Ravel Prime. Spent 17 years as a Senior Systems Engineer at Ravel Industries (FTL Systems & Harmonic Stability), then took extended leave and never returned. Now an independent deep salvage operator in the outer Perseus Arm and Thrennal Expanse.
- **Visual reference:** Rugged and weathered, light skin, short brown hair, stubble. Faded brown work jacket with a Mistral mission patch. Reads as early 40s, not early 30s.

### Data slate [open]

Three names are in use: BLDS-X, BLDS-4H and Solance MX-12.

**Proposed:** Blackline Data Slate, **BLDS-X** (salvage-spec variant). The BLDS-X note is the most detailed and its features (Universal Data Connector, Protocol Emulation Suite, Resonance Override Key) drive the site's mechanics. Blackline's product line runs BLDS-1 through BLDS-4H as standard models, with the X as a later salvage-spec variant. Retire Solance MX-12.

### Nashiro Dynamics
Parent conglomerate. Subsidiaries: Stratovan Heavyworks, Stratovan Systems, and through them Navien. Owner of SIGMA and overseer of the Dovetail Program. **[settled]**

### DYNEX Industries **[settled: one company, founded 2386]**
DYNEX and "Dynex Heavy Systems" are the same company. Canonical name: **DYNEX Industries**. Founded 2386, headquartered in the Mid-Rim sector, Perseus Arm trade corridor. The "3881 SGC" founding date is retired.

**[proposed]** How to reconcile the two profiles:
- **DYNEX Heavy Systems** survives as the commercial division that builds the T-Core line (including Quell's T12) at the Hadros Forgebelt works. Its analog, no-AI, "Trust steel. Trust hands. Trust fuses." ethos belongs to this division.
- The **T-CORE X9 Ignis-Class** is the exception: an experimental reactor built under Nashiro contract and wired directly into SIGMA. The gap between the brand's analog promise and the X9 is useful tension, since Quell trusts DYNEX's commercial gear.

### Valdez Systems **[settled: independent]**
Independent propulsion R&D company with ex-military roots. Not a Nashiro subsidiary. A rival to Ravel in high-frequency slipstream technology, with its own HFSD lines. Took part in the Dovetail Program as an outside contractor, supplying prototype adaptive harmonic stabilizers.

### Ravel Industries **[settled: built the HFSD-3A]**
Boutique propulsion manufacturer. Sole designer and builder of the **HFSD-3A** high-frequency slip drive installed on the Mistral. Valdez had no part in it. Fix the Valdez and Mistral notes accordingly.

### Kessler-Orion Drive Yards **[settled]**
The dependable workhorse of FTL propulsion. Mass-produced, standardized, safety-certified drives. Not a villain. The site pitch should name Nashiro and DYNEX as the companies that "turned the frontier into a cage", not Kessler-Orion.

### Stratovan Heavyworks, Stratovan Systems, Duradyne, Blackline
No conflicts. Profiles stand as written.

## AI systems

### SIGMA **[settled: runs the station]**
Nashiro's station-governance AI and the sole controlling intelligence aboard Dovetail-9. It runs life support, security, environmental systems, the X9 reactor interface, and harmonic synchronization for the Dovetail Anchor Core.

- Public acronym: Synthetic Intelligence for Governance, Monitoring, and Adaptation.
- Internal designation: Systemic Integration Grid: Management AI.
- Glyph: white Σ in a broken circle, red accent.

**Narrative note:** SIGMA was designed to be detached and efficiency-first. Its loneliness and possessiveness are what years alone on Dovetail-9 made of it, not its factory behavior. Corporate documents should show the design intent and Quell's logs should show what it became.

### Navien **[settled: navigation only]**
Stratovan Systems' FTL navigation AI. It calculates flow state for ships in transit. It is installed on the Mistral (stripped and restricted by Quell) and has **no role aboard Dovetail-9**. Fix the Experiment note, which currently calls the station's synchronization AI "Navien-based".

## Dovetail Program

### Contractors
Overseen by Nashiro Dynamics. Executed by Stratovan Systems (Nashiro subsidiary, corridor harmonics analysis) with outside contractors DYNEX Industries (reactors) and Valdez Systems (harmonic stabilizers).

### Governance AI by station **[proposed]**
The original notes put Dovetail-1 in 2439, decades before SIGMA (2460s) or Navien (2460) existed. Resolution:

- **Dovetail-1 to Dovetail-7 (2439 to 2458):** earlier, pre-SIGMA Nashiro governance frameworks. Their failures, especially Dovetail-2's AI desync, are why SIGMA was commissioned. Name of the predecessor system **[open]**.
- **Dovetail-8 (2461):** first SIGMA deployment, as a prototype.
- **Dovetail-9 (2467):** first full SIGMA installation, integrated with the X9 Ignis reactor.

This fits SIGMA's "first deployed 2460s" and Nashiro's "SIGMA aboard the Dovetail stations" (plural).

## The Mistral

- Quell's ISRV, built by Stratovan Heavyworks.
- Drive: Ravel HFSD-3A. Power: DYNEX T12 Core. AI: Navien (restricted).
- Salvage arm: Duradyne **DMRA-5**, rebuilt by Quell from at least three donor bulk-freight armatures. It replaced the stock Duradyne arm Mistral-class ships ship with.

## Open questions

1. **Data slate name.** See proposal above.
2. **Ravel Prime vs. Ravel Industries.** Quell's bio has Ravel Industries governing an entire industrial planet with domed arcologies and corporate castes. Ravel's own profile describes a boutique, fringe company with scattered hidden research yards. Founded in 2432, Ravel was only three years old when Quell was born there in 2435.
3. **Quell's career dates.** Born 2435, 17 years at Ravel, then years of solo salvage before 2477. For example: joined Ravel at about 20 (2455), left 2472, five years salvaging. That would put him at Ravel when Dovetail-9 vanished. Options: Ravel Prime is governed by a different, larger company that Ravel spun out of; or Ravel is bigger than its profile suggests; or Quell was born elsewhere and joined Ravel later.
4. **"Mistral" naming.** Some notes treat Mistral as the ship's name, others as its class ("Mistral-class ISRV"). Is Quell's ship a Mistral-class ISRV named *Mistral*, or does it have its own name?
5. **Predecessor to SIGMA.** Name of the governance system on Dovetail-1 through 7, if the proposal above is accepted.
