# Glossary

One meaning per term. Use these consistently in lore and documents.

**Who uses the jargon:** corporate and technical documents (Nashiro, DYNEX, Valdez, SIGMA's logs) use these terms freely. Crew emails and Quell's logs mostly don't: people say "the cold spot by the galley", not "RPE-0217".

## Travel

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Corridor** | A pathway through spacetime that FTL ships travel along. Made of time as well as space. |
| **Slipstream** | Industry word for FTL travel through a corridor. "Slipstream drive" and "slip drive" mean the engine. |
| **HFSD** | High-frequency slip drive: a drive built for unstable corridors. A type, made by Ravel and Valdez. The **HFSD-3A** is Ravel's model. |
| **Flow state** | The balance of frequency, trajectory, and velocity a ship must hold to travel a corridor safely. Navien manages it. |
| **Sliprunner** | A pirate or smuggler who runs corridors outside the law. Organized sliprunners hold Dovetail-7. |
| **Degraded corridor** | A corridor that has started to drift and become unstable. Ends in collapse. The Trillium Arc is one. |

## Corridor physics

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Harmonics** | A corridor's natural vibration pattern. Also the vibrations the array produces. |
| **Resonance** | Vibrating in step with something. The array works by resonating with a corridor's harmonics. |
| **Phase** | Where a vibration is in its cycle. Two things "in phase" are in step; "out of phase" means out of step. |

## Dovetail

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pin** | To hold one point of a corridor still. The **method**. |
| **Node** (phase node) | The point being held still. Inside it, time stops advancing. |
| **Stabilization** | The corridor steadied as a result of a pin. The **goal**. "Harmonic stabilization" is the experiment's formal name. |
| **Anchor** | A working, permanent pin. Dovetail-7 is the only documented one. |
| **Dovetail Anchor Core (DAC)** | The station's resonance array: the ~1.7 km structure that creates the pin. |
| **Counter-resonator** | The aft end of the array, balancing the forward focus. Forms no node. |

## The accident and after

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Cascade** (harmonic node cascade) | The spread of small secondary nodes through the station during the two seconds the shielding was down. |
| **Secondary node** | One of the small nodes the cascade left across the decks. Each holds a sliver of time. |
| **Temporal arrest** | Time stopped inside a node. |
| **Phase-displaced** | What happened to the crew: knocked out of normal time. |
| **Phase-lock** | Locking the array to a corridor's phase, the step before pinning. Dovetail-8 failed at it. |
| **Phase-locked** | The station's state since the accident: Dovetail-9 phase-locked so completely that the station itself was caught, out of step with normal space. Not "pinned". |
| **Alignment window** | A period when the corridor's drift brings the phase-locked station back in step with normal space. It is reachable, and signals get out. |
| **Echo** | Plain word for a trapped moment replaying. What crew and Quell would say. |
| **RPE** (residual phase event) | SIGMA's technical term for an echo, as logged. |
| **Ring** | What a node does when it replays: imprints a copy of its moment on the surrounding air. Each ring is fainter than the last. |
