# Dovetail-9 Crew

## Size **[settled]**

- **166 crew**, all aboard in 2467 and all phase-displaced. Raised from 97 when the hull grew to 400 m (see [[dovetail-9-station]]).
- Still sparse for the hull: much of the station is machinery SIGMA runs alone.

## Divisions **[settled]**

Seven divisions. Headcounts add up to 166.

| Division | Covers | Crew |
|---|---|---|
| **Command & Administration** | The station administrator, operations and comms, records. Senior staff hold Level 6 clearance. | 12 |
| **Security** | Small arms, access control, internal incidents only. No external defense (see [[dovetail-9-station]]). | 14 |
| **Medical** | Clinic and crew health, and tracking harmonic exposure: the Dovetail-5 lesson. | 8 |
| **Science** | Corridor harmonics research. Lead scientists (Level 6) and junior researchers, including those who kept their concerns private. | 45 |
| **Engineering** | The X9 and its chamber, the Anchor Core array, harmonic shielding, station power. | 40 |
| **Maintenance** | Life support, lifts, hydroponics, hull repair, and the suited crews who work the open array trusses. | 35 |
| **Habitation services** | Galley, stores, logistics, quarters. | 12 |

## Leadership

- **The station administrator** heads the station and answers to Nashiro off-station (see [[the-secret]], Chain of command).
- **[proposed]** The "director" named among the Level 6 senior staff in [[the-secret]] is the **Science Director**, head of the Science division: a different person from the administrator.

## Contractor teams **[proposed]**

Some teams work for the program's contractors rather than Nashiro, embedded in a division:

- **DYNEX Industries** staff run the X9, within Engineering.
- **Valdez Systems** staff run the harmonic shielding, within Engineering.
- **Stratovan Systems** analysts do corridor harmonics analysis, within Science.

Different employers and loyalties side by side are material for crew emails, especially around the accident and the shielding.
