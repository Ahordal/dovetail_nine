# Ravel/ProTek Model 3A EVA Suit

**Quell's pressure suit.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Make** | Ravel/ProTek Model 3A |
| **Type** | Ravel-class exterior suit for solo extravehicular operations |

## Description

A modified Ravel-class exterior suit. Reinforced seals, field-patched tears, redundant seals, and reinforced magnetic boots. The corporate insignia has been stripped. Mismatched components suggest years of field customization.

## Quell's modifications

- Independent thermal control
- Analog override systems
- Functional, unpolished, survival-first

## Role in the story

- A leftover from his Ravel years, with the insignia removed: he kept the gear and dropped the company.
- What he wears to work the Anchor Core array's open trusses, which only suited crews could reach.

## Open

- **ProTek** isn't profiled. Possibly a suit maker that co-built Ravel's crew suits.
