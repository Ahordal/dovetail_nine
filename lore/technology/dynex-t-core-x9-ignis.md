# DYNEX T-CORE X9 Ignis-Class

**Class 5+ singularity containment reactor. Power source of Dovetail-9 and its Anchor Core.**

## The name

| Part | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **DYNEX** | Manufacturer: [[dynex-industries]] |
| **T-CORE** | Product line: the Torsion-Core series of high-density energy systems |
| **X9** | Ninth-generation prototype in the experimental T-CORE line |
| **Ignis-Class** | Singularity-based containment reactors. "Ignis" is Latin for fire, a nod to harnessing stellar power. |

## Specifications

| | |
|---|---|
| **Application** | FTL corridor stabilization infrastructure |
| **Install location** | Core Chamber, Dovetail-9 |
| **Installed** | 2467, as part of the Dovetail Program |
| **Dimensions** | 4.5 m × 2.5 m × 2.3 m |
| **Mass** | ~26 metric tons installed |
| **Output** | 3.5 exawatts sustained; 7.8 exawatts surge threshold |
| **Startup charge** | 420 petajoules |
| **Runtime** | Theoretically indefinite |

## Containment & systems

- **Primary containment:** Graviton Lattice Shell
- **Secondary systems:** Hawking Harvest Rings, Magnetic Flux Confinement, Quantum Coolant Matrix
- **Core housing:** Argonax Fiber Shell (proprietary DYNEX material)

## Failsafes & limitations

- **ZK-Class Override:** 2 kg matter/antimatter injection. The controlled worst case.
- **Failsafe destruction radius:** ~3.2 km
- **Shutdown option:** none. Irreversible once initialized.
- **Containment draw:** 1.3 exawatts continuous
- **No physical access** during runtime
- **Only two built.** Unit 001 aboard Dovetail-9. Unit 002 under classified lockdown.

## Field anecdotes

- Known to **destabilize under prolonged harmonic exposure**. In DYNEX's own notes.
- Engineers nicknamed it "the star in the box".

## Role in the story

- **Why it can't stop:** a pinned corridor has to stay pinned, so its power source can never shut down. The failsafe is the only way to end it.
- **The accident:** harmonic feedback from the Anchor Core made it surge toward its 7.8 EW threshold. The containment-priority protocol pulled every reserve, backups included, to hold containment, and the shielding dropped for two seconds. See [[the-accident]].
- **Why containment outranks everything:** losing containment means an uncontrolled singularity with no known limit. The ZK-Class failsafe is the *good* outcome.
- **The ending:** Quell triggers the ZK-Class failsafe after sending his data dump.
- **Unit 002** is Quell's motive for the dump: Nashiro could try again.
- The exception to DYNEX Heavy Systems' analog, no-AI ethos: built under Nashiro contract and wired directly into SIGMA.
