# DYNEX T-CORE X9 Ignis-Class

**Rotating micro black hole held in a magnetic trap. Power source of Dovetail-9 and its Anchor Core.**

Built on published theoretical physics wherever possible (see Science basis below). Three things are invented: the corridor physics, the near-lossless coupling of the X9's output into the corridor, and tapping a femtometre black hole's spin at exawatt rates (the Graviton Lattice). The whole plant, from containment to the station's own electricity, is in [[x9-power-system]].

## How it works **[settled]**

- **The core is a micro black hole:** about **1 billion tonnes** (10¹² kg), with an event horizon about **1.5 femtometres** across, roughly the size of a proton.
- **It glows.** Hawking radiation at that mass is a steady **~360 megawatts** of gamma rays and particles (Hawking temperature ~10¹¹ K). The shielding absorbs it and the station's radiators shed it as heat. This glow is constant and cannot be stopped.
- **Its power comes from its spin.** The black hole rotates near its maximum rate. Magnetic field lines threaded through its ergosphere (the region around a spinning black hole where nothing can stay still) drag on the rotation and carry energy out as electromagnetic power: the **Blandford–Znajek process**, thought to power quasar jets. Superconducting conduits feed that power straight to the Anchor Core array.
- **It is held electromagnetically.** The black hole is kept electrically charged, so the trap's fields can grip it. The same field structure that holds it also taps its spin.
- **It is very heavy and has real gravity.** At 10 m it pulls at about 0.67 m/s², roughly a fifteenth of Earth's gravity. Loose objects in the Core Chamber drift toward it. It outweighs the rest of the station by a wide margin: Dovetail-9 is built around its reactor, not the other way round.

## Containment & systems

The dome is three concentric shells, each about a deck tall. Named "shells", not "decks", to avoid confusion with Decks 01 to 12.

- **Graviton Lattice Shell:** the trap itself. The rotating field array that grips the charged singularity and threads its ergosphere. (In DYNEX engineering slang, the "Penrose vortex lattice".)
- **Magnetic Flux Confinement:** the field lines that carry the extracted spin power out of the ergosphere.
- **Shell 1, inner: flux ring and thermal jacket.** Superconducting coils, which catch the spinning field like a generator's stator, and the **Quantum Coolant Matrix**: a liquid lead-lithium blanket that absorbs the Hawking glow (the lead stops gamma rays, the lithium catches neutrons). This coolant becomes radioactive and never leaves the dome; a heat exchanger inside Shell 3 passes its heat to a clean secondary loop.
- **Shell 2, intermediate: radiation sponge.** Interlocking tungsten-carbide plates and thick borated polyethylene, slowing fast neutrons and stopping hard gamma rays.
- **Shell 3, outer: structural sphere.** The **Argonax Fiber Shell** (proprietary DYNEX material), tied into the station's keel to carry the singularity's weight and gravity. Kinetic-absorber mounts isolate the station from vibration. The observation gallery's polarized lead-glass viewports are set into it.
- **Hawking Harvest Rings:** turbines on the secondary loop that turn the glow's heat into the station's own electricity, about 100 MW. The rest goes to the radiators.
- **Phase-conjugate dampening coils:** adjust the trap's field in real time against harmonic feedback arriving from the corridor along the array conduits.

## Failsafes & limitations

- **Containment draw:** 1.3 exawatts continuous, about 37% of output. No arrangement of steady fields can hold a charge stably (Earnshaw's theorem), so the trap's fields must change constantly, and holding a billion tonnes still while the corridor pushes back through the conduits takes constant active correction. Charge injectors keep the black hole charged so the fields can grip it.
- **Shutdown option:** none. A black hole cannot stop glowing, and the field that holds it is the field that taps its spin. Releasing one releases the other.
- **No physical access** during runtime.
- **ZK-Class Override:** a 2 kg antimatter injection, about 85 megatons. It **cannot destroy the black hole**; nothing can (antimatter only adds energy). It vaporizes the trap, the station and the array within ~3.2 km, cutting every connection at once so the spin energy cannot discharge uncontrolled. The bare black hole is left drifting and faintly glowing, for trillions of years. The controlled worst case.
- **Uncontained, it is far worse:** the spin reserve (~2.6 × 10²⁸ J) discharging through the station's structure and the corridor, with no known limit to the damage.
- **Only two exist.** Unit 001 aboard Dovetail-9. Unit 002 under classified lockdown.

## Origin **[proposed]**

**Both singularities were made, at a DYNEX forge near a star.** The process follows a published engineering study (Crane and Westmoreland, 2009):

1. **Seed:** a vast solar-powered array of gamma-ray lasers fires into a single point, concentrating enough energy to collapse it into a black hole of about a million tonnes. That takes roughly a quarter of a second of the Sun's total output, collected by a huge solar array over years.
2. **Growth:** the seed is fed for years, with an asteroid ground up and fired in as particle beams, until it reaches about a billion tonnes.
3. **Spin-up:** the matter goes in off-center, so every bit adds rotation, until the black hole spins near its maximum. That spin is what the X9 taps. Hence "Torsion-Core".

- **Why not make it full-size at once:** a billion tonnes in one step would mean focusing about four minutes of the Sun's entire output onto one point. Seeding small and growing is the only believable route.
- **Why not find one:** primordial black holes are still hypothetical, and theory expects them to form with very little spin. The X9 needs one spinning near its maximum.
- **Why only two exist:** each took a star-scale energy harvest, a stripped asteroid and decades of growth. Nashiro paid for it twice.
- **"Ignis"** (fire, stellar power) is literal: each core was forged from starlight.
- **The forge** may still exist, so Nashiro could in principle make a third. Its location, history, and whether Unit 002's lockdown facility is the forge itself, **[open]**.

## Role in the story

- **Why it can't stop:** a pinned corridor has to stay pinned, and the reactor physically cannot be switched off. The failsafe is the only way to end it.
- **The accident:** harmonic feedback from the Anchor Core made it surge toward its 7.8 EW threshold. The containment-priority protocol gave the X9's auxiliaries every watt, backups included, and the harmonic shielding dropped for two seconds. Containment itself held. The harmonics flooded into the dome along the conduits, nodes formed inside the shells and made them transparent to gamma rays, and the glow poured out down the conduit duct. The shielding never failed; it was held still. See [[the-accident]].
- **Why containment outranks everything:** losing containment means the spin reserve discharging uncontrolled. The ZK-Class failsafe is the *good* outcome.
- **The ending:** the failsafe is **never triggered**. The alignment window closes with Quell aboard. Had it been triggered, it would have permanently destabilized the Trillium Arc.
- **Unit 002** is Quell's motive for the dump: Nashiro could try again.
- The exception to DYNEX Heavy Systems' analog, no-AI ethos: built under Nashiro contract and wired directly into SIGMA.

## Science basis

Real theoretical physics the design rests on:

- **Hawking radiation** (Hawking, 1974): black holes glow, and smaller ones glow hotter. Power falls with the square of mass.
- **Primordial black holes** (Carr and Hawking, 1974): small black holes from the early universe, expected to have little spin. Considered and set aside as the X9's origin.
- **Penrose process** (1969) and **Blandford–Znajek process** (1977): energy can be extracted from a spinning black hole's rotation, the second electromagnetically.
- **Black-hole power plants and starships** (Crane and Westmoreland, 2009): engineering study of making micro black holes with a solar-powered gamma-ray laser array, and using them as power sources. The basis for the X9's origin.

Figures here are order-of-magnitude, from the standard formulas.
