# Kestral RS-9V Resonance Scanner

**Quell's handheld harmonic scanner.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Manufacturer** | Kestral (no profile yet) |
| **Model** | RS-9V |
| **Type** | Handheld, directional harmonic scanner |

## What it does

Detects micro-distortions in structural materials and in corridor residue fields: shifts in harmonics too small for station sensors to flag. Optimized for deep salvage and for recognizing corridor decay patterns. Used to find corridor echo patterns, failed FTL nodes, and unstable power fields.

## Quell's unit

- He **disabled the auto-calibration** and runs it entirely by feel.
- He rarely trusts station readings. The scanner is how he "hears the truth".

## Role in the story

- **His way of detecting echoes.** Under [[phase-mechanics]], a secondary node is a pocket of held tension. A harmonic scanner should pick it up as a faint, constant distortion, and spike when it rings.
- That makes him one of the few people equipped to notice what SIGMA logs as RPEs, and to cross-check SIGMA's data against his own.
- Its readings could appear in the data dump alongside his logs, as a second instrument record beside SIGMA's.
